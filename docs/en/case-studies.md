# Case studies: *Homem na Estrada* and *Vidas Secas*

Two complete instantiation examples. [`examples/case-studies.ttl`](../../examples/case-studies.ttl) holds both works together; each work also has its own self-contained file: [`example_homem_na_estrada.ttl`](../../examples/example_homem_na_estrada.ttl) and [`example_vidas_secas.ttl`](../../examples/example_vidas_secas.ttl). They are generated by `python scripts/split_examples.py`, which copies shared patterns, cases and sources into each file. The studies answer four research questions in SPARQL ([`queries/RQ-*.rq`](../../queries/)).

| Work | Medium | Selections | Derivation |
|---|---|---|---|
| *Homem na Estrada*, Racionais MC's, album *Raio X do Brasil* (1993), written by Mano Brown | song (rap) | 15 events located by line ranges of the lyrics | sample of *Ela Partiu* (Tim Maia) |
| *Vidas Secas*, Graciliano Ramos (1938) | novel | 4 chapters (Mudança, Cadeia, Contas, Fuga) | film adaptation by Nelson Pereira dos Santos (1963) |

## Method and limits

- **No lyrics or novel text are stored.** Each selection is one *event* of the narrative. In the song it is located by the lines where it happens (usually 2 to 8), in the lyric transcription supplied by the researcher. In the novel the selection is a chapter.
- **Situation descriptions are paraphrases representing one analyst's reading** (`InterpretationRecord`), not the content of the work. `aro:confidenceScore` gives how sure that reading is.
- **Empirical cases cite real, public sources**, with URLs checked when the example was written.
- **Correspondence claims state similarity or possibility.** They do not claim that the work narrates a specific real event, nor that there is causation. Each evidence assessment says what it supports and what it does not.

**Plural readings.** Event 15 has a recorded justification and a recorded challenge:
- **Justification:** the sequence police raid → death contrasts with the police version, and the reading takes that version as criminalizing the victim.
- **Challenge** (`Challenge`): the lyrics leave the killers implicit, so the report's version cannot be excluded within the narrative itself.

Real institutions and places that appear in the lyrics are represented as `DepictedElement`: FEBEM, IBGE, IML and the M'Boi Mirim road.

The readings cite academic analyses of the song: the [Intexto/UFRGS article](https://seer.ufrgs.br/index.php/intexto/article/view/150581) and the [UFPB thesis](https://repositorio.ufpb.br/jspui/handle/123456789/31862) on police violence in the song.

**Creation and derivation:** Mano Brown (Pedro Paulo Soares Pereira; author and lyricist) and Racionais MC's (performers) appear as external agents (DBpedia). The track samples Tim Maia's *Ela Partiu*, according to the [album article](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raio-X_do_Brasil).

## Vidas Secas — chapters and reading

| Chapter | Represented situation (reading) | Pattern |
|---|---|---|
| 1 «Mudança» | A family of rural workers crosses the dry backlands looking for somewhere to survive | Drought-driven forced migration |
| 3 «Cadeia» | A soldier provokes Fabiano, who is arrested, beaten and jailed overnight without having committed a crime | Arbitrary or lethal violence by state agents |
| 10 «Contas» | The landowner's accounts, with interest Fabiano cannot check, keep him in debt | Exploitation through debt and opaque accounts |
| 13 «Fuga» | With a new drought the family leaves, hoping for a better life in the southern cities | Drought-driven forced migration |

**Derivation:** Nelson Pereira dos Santos's film (1963) is represented as an `IntermedialAdaptation` in a serious regime. The medium is recorded as modified and the situations as retained, including the state-violence and drought-migration patterns (confidence 0.7). The claim cites the [film article](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barren_Lives_(film)).

## Patterns and real-world correspondences

| Pattern | Works | Real case | Source | What the assessment supports |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Arbitrary or lethal violence by state security agents against a poor man | both | Deaths from police interventions in Brazil | [FBSP, Yearbook 2021](https://forumseguranca.org.br/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/4-as-mortes-decorrentes-de-intervencao-policial-no-brasil-em-2020.pdf) | that the pattern is real and recurrent; not that a work narrates a real event |
| same | both | Carandiru massacre (1992) | [encyclopedia](https://pt.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massacre_do_Carandiru) | a documented instance of the pattern; no work is claimed to describe it |
| Barriers to reintegration after prison | *Homem na Estrada* | Recidivism and reintegration of former prisoners | [IPEA, 2015](https://repositorio.ipea.gov.br/bitstream/11058/7510/1/RP_Reincid%c3%aancia_2015.pdf) | partial: the report does not measure job refusal due to a record |
| Precarious urban housing without sanitation or water | *Homem na Estrada* | Lack of sewage collection in Brazil | [Poder360 / Trata Brasil](https://www.poder360.com.br/brasil/100-milhoes-de-brasileiros-nao-tem-coleta-de-esgotos-em-casa/) | national scale (2017 data); not a specific neighbourhood |
| Drought-driven forced migration | *Vidas Secas* | Camps for drought refugees in Ceará (1932) | [FAPESP](https://revistapesquisa.fapesp.br/memorias-da-seca/), [Diário do Nordeste](https://diariodonordeste.verdesmares.com.br/ceara/campo-de-concentracao-que-abrigou-16-mil-retirantes-no-ceara-deve-ter-predios-e-ruinas-tombados-1.3258221) | historical documentation; no fictional family is placed in the camps |

A correspondence links a **pattern** to a real case, not a work. The state-violence pattern is shared by both works (the police raid and death in *Homem na Estrada*; Fabiano beaten and jailed by the yellow soldier in *Vidas Secas*), so its two cases, police lethality in 2020 and Carandiru in 1992, are reached from both works and from the film that retains the pattern. They state that the **type of situation** is documented, not that the 1938 novel or the 1993 song depicts those events. For this reason the texts of correspondences and assessments mention only the pattern; what belongs to one work stays in its interpretation records.

Five song patterns **have no real case yet** and remain open research points:
- absence of public services in the periphery;
- institutionalization of poor youth;
- lynching;
- child hunger;
- violence of the retail drug trade.

The debt-exploitation pattern of *Vidas Secas* also has no case yet.

There is also an `ExplanationHypothesis` for the state-violence pattern ("selective policing of poor and Black populations"). It is represented as a hypothesis, not as an established cause.

## Research questions and answers

To run:

```bash
python scripts/query.py examples/case-studies.ttl --query queries/RQ-01-passages-to-real-cases.rq
```

| ID | Research question | Answer in the case studies |
|---|---|---|
| RQ-01 | Which passages of a work represent situations claimed to correspond to documented real cases, and where can the source be consulted? | 18 rows: 9 song events (with line ranges) and 3 novel chapters linked to 5 real cases, each with a URL, in narrative order. |
| RQ-02 | Which situation patterns recur across independent works? | **Arbitrary or lethal violence by state agents** occurs in both works. |
| RQ-03 | How is each correspondence supported (evidence, source, assessment)? | 6 rows, each with the evidence, the URL and the assessment of what is and is not supported. |
| RQ-04 | Which derivations retain a source pattern that corresponds to real cases? | The film adaptation of *Vidas Secas* retains state violence (2 real cases) and drought migration (1 case). |

The tests (`python tests/test_project.py`) check these answers.

## Continue in the annotator

```bash
python scripts/annotator.py
```

Load `examples/case-studies.ttl` with **Load .ttl**. Then:
- on the "Lyric text" expression, use **Attach .txt** with your copy of the lyrics: each event then shows its lines;
- add events or adjust line ranges;
- review or challenge the readings with a `Challenge`;
- add cases and sources, especially for the patterns that have none yet;
- save a new file.
