# Purpose, scope and intended uses

**What:** Ontology of artistic objects, selected segments, plural interpretations, represented situations, situation patterns, **derivation between works** (translation, cover, sampling, adaptation, parody, fan work…) and claims of correspondence with empirical reality.

**Why:** Make interpretations traceable, comparable and contestable without mistaking fictional depiction for verified real-world occurrence.

**For what:** Research, cataloguing, cross-media comparative analysis, evidence-linked interpretation and knowledge graphs.

**In scope:** Music, video, images, visual art, texts and other media; selectable portions; interpretive arguments; reusable references (news, scientific papers, testimonies, documents and datasets); corroboration and disagreement; criteria; derivation claims at artwork, expression, manifestation or selection level, what each derivation retains or transforms, creative agents and roles, and the explanation (justification, sources, evidence) of each claim. Derivative works are treated as creations in their own right.

**Out of scope (v0.2):** Auto-extraction of text/media content, fact-checking news automatically, establishing definitive interpretations, automatic causal inference, all of UFO-C formal semantics, any specific artistic case, automatic similarity detection, representation of legality, originality or infringement, and inferred (transitive) derivation chains.

**Epistemic commitment:** The research programme assumes works can be investigated for relations to reality; any particular correspondence is a fallible claim requiring explicit evidence and scope.
